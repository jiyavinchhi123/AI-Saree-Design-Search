How Color-Invariant Matching Works

The AI Saree Design Engine eliminates chromatic channels (Hue & Saturation) and decomposes the image into a 3-channel structural tensor comprising CLAHE contrast-normalized luminance, Sobel gradient edge magnitudes (for motif contours and temple spires), and adaptive texture channels (for jacquard weave patterns). This tensor is processed through a deep MobileNetV3 visual backbone combined with spatial HOG zoning, allowing the exact same design in completely different colorways (e.g., Red vs. Peacock Blue) to match with >95% similarity!
